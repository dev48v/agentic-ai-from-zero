# Nimbus Cloud — Regions and Data Residency

Nimbus Cloud runs in four regions: US East (Virginia), US West (Oregon), Europe (Frankfurt), and Asia Pacific (Singapore).

Each customer picks a home region when the account is created. All primary data is stored in the home region and never leaves it, which lets European customers keep data inside the EU for GDPR data-residency requirements.

Backups are replicated to a second region within the same jurisdiction: US accounts replicate between the two US regions, and the Frankfurt region replicates to a second EU facility. The Singapore region does not currently have a paired backup region, so Asia Pacific backups are stored within Singapore only.

Moving an existing account to a different home region is a manual migration handled by support and can take up to 48 hours.
