# Nimbus Cloud — Uptime SLA and Support

The signed uptime SLA is available only on the Enterprise plan. Nimbus guarantees 99.95 percent monthly uptime for Enterprise customers.

If monthly uptime falls below 99.95 percent, Enterprise customers receive service credits: a 10 percent credit for uptime between 99.0 and 99.95 percent, and a 25 percent credit for uptime below 99.0 percent.

Support response targets depend on the plan. Starter customers get community forum support only. Team customers get email support with a one business-day first-response target. Enterprise customers get priority support with a one-hour first-response target for critical incidents, 24 hours a day.

Scheduled maintenance windows are announced at least 72 hours in advance and do not count against the uptime SLA.
