# Nimbus Cloud — Plans and Pricing

Nimbus Cloud is billed per seat, per month, with no setup fee. There are three plans.

The Starter plan costs 19 US dollars per seat each month and includes up to 5 seats, 50 GB of object storage, and community support only.

The Team plan costs 49 US dollars per seat each month, allows up to 50 seats, includes 500 GB of object storage, and adds single sign-on and email support.

The Enterprise plan costs 99 US dollars per seat each month, has no seat cap, includes 5 TB of object storage, and adds a dedicated account manager, audit logging, and a signed uptime SLA.

Annual billing is discounted 20 percent versus paying month to month. Nimbus does not offer a permanent free tier, but every plan starts with a 14-day free trial that requires no credit card.
